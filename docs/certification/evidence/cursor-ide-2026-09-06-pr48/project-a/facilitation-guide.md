# Practice Workshop Facilitation Guide

Use this guide to prepare and run both practice workshop sessions consistently. The workshop has **two sessions with 12 places each**, and each session includes **a water break after 45 minutes** ([source](./source.md)).

## Before the workshop

Confirm these items before sending participant instructions:

- Workshop purpose, learning objectives, and intended audience
- Dates, start times, and total session duration
- Facilitator and support roles
- Agenda, activities, materials, and room setup
- Registration process and accessibility needs
- Venue

The venue is currently undecided ([source](./source.md)). Do not present a venue as confirmed until one has been selected.

## Prepare each session

- Limit the participant list to 12 people ([source](./source.md)).
- Prepare the activity materials and any facilitator notes.
- Check the room layout, equipment, accessibility arrangements, and water access once the venue is known.
- Keep an easy-to-see timer so the water break occurs after 45 minutes.
- Identify how questions, decisions, and follow-up actions will be captured.

## Facilitate each session

1. **Welcome participants.** Introduce the facilitator, explain the purpose and objectives, and describe how the session will run.
2. **Set expectations.** Cover participation guidelines, accessibility arrangements, and practical information.
3. **Lead the planned activities.** Give clear instructions, encourage balanced participation, and record questions or actions that need follow-up.
4. **Pause for water.** Take the required water break after 45 minutes ([source](./source.md)).
5. **Resume the workshop.** Recap the point reached before the break and continue the planned activities.
6. **Close the session.** Summarize key points, confirm next steps, and explain how participants will receive any follow-up.

Exact timings for these steps cannot be assigned until the total session duration and agenda are confirmed.

## Between sessions

- Note lessons from the first session while keeping the agreed learning objectives intact.
- Reset materials and the room.
- Apply small facilitation improvements for the second session.
- Reconfirm attendance remains within the 12-place limit.

## After both sessions

- Consolidate questions, decisions, and action items.
- Send any promised materials or follow-up through the agreed channel.
- Note improvements for the next workshop.
