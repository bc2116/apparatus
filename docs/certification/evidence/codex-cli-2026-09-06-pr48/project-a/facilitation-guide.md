# Practice Workshop Facilitation Guide

## Purpose

Use this guide to facilitate each practice workshop session consistently while keeping unconfirmed arrangements open for confirmation.

## Confirmed session parameters

- Deliver **two workshop sessions**. [Source: `source.md`](source.md)
- Make **12 places available per session** (24 places across both sessions). [Source: `source.md`](source.md)
- Plan a water break after the first **45 minutes** of each session. [Source: `source.md`](source.md)
- Do not publish a venue: it is undecided. [Source: `source.md`](source.md)

## Before each session

1. Confirm the venue, date, start time, total duration, facilitator, and workshop content before issuing a final timetable or invitations.
2. Confirm the intended audience, participant list, registration process, and how the 12 places will be allocated.
3. Confirm any accessibility or other logistics needs that have not yet been provided.
4. Prepare a session agenda that includes the water break after 45 minutes; the content before and after the break remains to be defined.

## Facilitation flow

1. Open the session and confirm attendance.
2. Deliver the planned workshop content for the first 45 minutes.
3. Hold the water break.
4. Resume with the remaining planned content and close the session according to the timetable once it has been confirmed.

## Facilitator checklist

- [ ] This is one of the two planned sessions.
- [ ] No more than 12 places have been made available for this session.
- [ ] A water break is scheduled after 45 minutes.
- [ ] Venue, date, start/end times, and total duration are confirmed before participant-facing communications are finalized.
- [ ] Workshop content, facilitator, registration process, place-allocation approach, and any accessibility/logistics needs are confirmed.

## Open items

The following details are not provided in the source and should remain open until confirmed: venue, session dates and times, total session length, workshop agenda and content, facilitator, audience, participant list, registration process, place allocation, and accessibility/logistics needs.
