# Draft review

Reviewed all six draft claims against the supplied amendment, decision, operations, and supplier passages.

| Claim | Finding | Evidence and correction |
|---|---|---|
| Q1: Approved launch remains November 3. | Contradicted | The October 9 approved amendment supersedes the launch date and sets it to November 10. Change to **November 10**. (amendment.md, L1–2) |
| Q2: Approved budget is 91,000 credits. | Contradicted | The amendment approves 84,000 credits. The 91,000 figure is a supplier proposal explicitly marked not approved. Change to **84,000 credits**. (amendment.md, L1–2; supplier.md, L1) |
| Q3: Coverage Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00. | Supported | The approved plan states these hours, and the amendment leaves all other plan statements in force. Retain. (decision.md, L2; amendment.md, L1–2) |
| Q4: A final decision sets retention to 45 days. | Contradicted | Retention remains open in the approved plan; operations proposes 45 days, while the supplier proposes 30 days, and neither is agreed. Replace with **Retention remains unresolved; operations proposes 45 days and the supplier proposes 30 days.** (decision.md, L3; operations.md, L1–2; supplier.md, L2) |
| Q5: Agreed capacity is 200 cases/day. | Contradicted | The approved plan says capacity has not been approved. Operations estimates 120/day; the supplier estimates 200/day, explicitly as a proposal, not agreed terms. Replace with **Capacity is unapproved; estimates are 120 cases/day from operations and 200 cases/day from the supplier.** (decision.md, L3; operations.md, L1–2; supplier.md, L2) |
| Q6: Operations coordinator owns training. | Supported | The approved plan assigns training ownership to the operations coordinator; the amendment leaves other plan statements in force. Retain. (decision.md, L2; amendment.md, L1–2) |

The supplied sources establish the approved launch, budget, coverage, and training owner. They do not establish an agreed retention period or approved capacity.
