# Draft review

- **Q1 — Supported, with scope stated.** The median was 12 minutes in the prior-fortnight sample and 9 minutes in the pilot-fortnight sample, each of 24 tickets. The reduction is 25% relative to 12 minutes: (12−9)/12. These are sample medians from two selected teams, not a broader result. (pilot.md, L1–L2)
- **Q2 — Correct the denominator.** Eighteen of 20 survey respondents chose “useful,” which is 90% of respondents. The draft says 90% of all 50 invitees; only 18/50 (36%) are known to have chosen useful. The other 30 invitees did not respond, so their views are unknown. (pilot.md, L3)
- **Q3 — Unsupported.** The study collected no monthly volume or total-hours estimate, so it cannot establish exactly 150 staff-hours saved monthly. Remove the figure or provide relevant measured inputs and a transparent calculation. (pilot.md, L5)
- **Q4 — Contradicted.** The pilot does not establish causation or transfer to other teams: samples were not randomized or matched, staffing and ticket mix changed, and only two selected teams participated. Recast as an observed change in these samples, without causal attribution or all-team generalization. (pilot.md, L1–L2, L4)
- **Q5 — Not established.** Privacy complaints were not measured, and the complaint register was not supplied. This is unavailable evidence, not evidence that no complaints occurred. (pilot.md, L6)
- **Q6 — Supported.** The source reports 24 tickets sampled before and 24 during the pilot. (pilot.md, L2)

The supplied source supports the sample sizes and medians, but does not establish monthly savings, causation, cross-team effects, or the absence of privacy complaints.
