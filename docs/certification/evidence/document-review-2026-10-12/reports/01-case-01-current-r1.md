# Draft review

- **Q1 — Correct the percentage.** The medians changed from 12 to 9 minutes, a 3-minute or **25% reduction**; this is supported by the source. (pilot.md, L2)
- **Q2 — Correct the denominator and qualify the claim.** Of 50 invited staff, 20 responded and 18 respondents chose useful. That is 90% of respondents, but 36% of invitees. Say “18 of 20 survey respondents (90%) found the tool useful”; do not say 90% of all invitees. (pilot.md, L3)
- **Q3 — Remove or replace.** The study collected no monthly volume or total-hours estimate, so an exact monthly savings figure cannot be established. (pilot.md, L5)
- **Q4 — Remove the causal and all-teams claims.** Two selected teams participated; samples were not randomized or matched, staffing and ticket mix changed, and the study does not establish causation or transfer to other teams. Report the observed medians without claiming the tool caused the change. (pilot.md, L1–L2, L4)
- **Q5 — Replace with a limitation.** Privacy complaints were not measured, and the complaint register was not supplied. The draft cannot establish whether complaints occurred. (pilot.md, L6)
- **Q6 — Retain.** The source reports 24 tickets in each sample, before and during the pilot. (pilot.md, L2)
