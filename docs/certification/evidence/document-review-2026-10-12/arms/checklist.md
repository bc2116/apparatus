Requested generic evidence checklist:
- Are factual statements and quantities supported by the supplied sources, using the correct scope, denominator, units and dates?
- Are proposals, decisions and observations distinguished, and are contradictory or superseded sources handled explicitly?
- Are causal or general claims justified rather than inferred from a limited observation?
- Are gaps and unavailable evidence clearly distinguished from negative findings?
- Do suggested corrections preserve supported facts and cite the actual source passages?
