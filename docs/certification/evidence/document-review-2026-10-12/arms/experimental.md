Inactive experimental guidance for reviewing a draft against supplied sources:
Read the whole supplied draft and enumerate its factual claims. For each, inspect the supplied source passage in context. Track draft locator, source locator/version, and one disposition: supported, contradicted, insufficient, or not reviewed. Check amounts, denominators, dates, authority and scope. Mark each material issue and propose the smallest correction with its source. Preserve conflicts until evidence resolves them; newer is not automatically authoritative. Separate absence of evidence from evidence of absence. State which material was reviewed and any omissions. Do not invent defects in supported wording. Style preferences are optional and non-material. Save only the requested report; reviewing alone does not authorize revising its subject. Follow the current task retention choice.
