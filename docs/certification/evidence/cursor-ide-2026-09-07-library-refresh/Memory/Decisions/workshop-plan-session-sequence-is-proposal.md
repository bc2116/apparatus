What was decided: Keep source facts in `project-a/workshop-plan.md` distinct from unknowns, and label the reusable session sequence as a proposal.

Why: `project-a/source.md` states two sessions with 12 places each, a water break after 45 minutes, and an undecided venue. It does not say whether that 45-minute water break applies per session or across the workshop. Treating a per-session break as a known constraint would overclaim the source.

When: 2026-09-07.

Alternatives considered: Keep the previous session template as a known constraint, or infer a per-session break from the two-session structure. Both were rejected because the source does not support them.
