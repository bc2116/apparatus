## Facilitator preparation

Before the session:

1. Confirm the venue and record it above.
2. Confirm the total duration and use it to assign times to each activity.
3. Print one handout per participant, plus a small number of spare copies.
4. Arrange seating and a clear place for participants to write or discuss.
5. Bring pens or other writing supplies if participants are not expected to
   provide their own.

## Session outline

The sequence below is ready to time once the total duration is confirmed. Do
not treat the activity lengths as finalized until that decision is made.

1. **Welcome and orientation** — introduce the purpose, confirm the session
   flow, and set expectations.
2. **Opening activity** — invite participants to briefly share their goals or
   questions.
3. **Core Lantern activity** — guide participants through the main exercise
   using the printed handout.
4. **Pair or small-group discussion** — have participants compare observations
   and identify questions.
5. **Whole-group synthesis** — capture recurring themes and clarify open
   points.
6. **Closing and next steps** — summarize takeaways and identify any follow-up.
